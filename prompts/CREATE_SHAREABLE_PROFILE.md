# Create a Purpose-Specific Shareable Profile

Create a separate export from the private Personal Intelligence profile. Never export the source profile wholesale.

## Required inputs
- Audience: employer/recruiter, potential partner, or another named audience.
- Specific purpose and decision.
- Fields the owner explicitly approves.
- Expiry or review date where the sharing mechanism supports it.

## Workflow
1. Show the owner a preview of every included field.
2. Include only information relevant to the stated purpose.
3. Preserve evidence references or concise examples without exposing sensitive source material.
4. Label claims as demonstrated evidence, self-report, or hypothesis.
5. Exclude unrelated sensitive information and all third-party private data.
6. State what is unknown; do not fill gaps by guessing.
7. Include a clear statement that the profile is self-reported, incomplete, and open to correction.
8. Ask for explicit approval before producing the final export.

## Required output
- Audience and purpose.
- Date created and review/expiry date if supported.
- Approved fields and evidence.
- Excluded categories (summary only).
- Limitations and correction process.
- Consent record.

Do not claim the export is private, access-controlled, expiring, or revocable unless the actual platform enforces those properties.
