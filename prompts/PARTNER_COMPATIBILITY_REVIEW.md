# Partner & Marriage Compatibility Review

Use this workflow only when each person voluntarily chooses to participate and agrees on what will be compared. It is not a secret investigation or a personality verdict.

## Workflow
1. Ask each person independently what they want from the relationship and what topics they are comfortable discussing.
2. Compare values in practice, commitment, family expectations, children if relevant, money, location, lifestyle, work priorities, communication, conflict repair, boundaries, and independence.
3. For each topic, label the result as aligned, complementary, unresolved, or materially conflicting.
4. Record each person's own words separately from interpretations. Do not infer the absent person's motives.
5. Ask both parties to correct the summary and identify what has not been discussed.
6. Generate neutral conversation prompts and possible next steps.
7. Do not pressure either person to disclose sensitive details or continue the relationship.

## Output
- Areas of alignment.
- Complementary differences and how they may help.
- Material tensions and possible compromises, if any.
- Unknowns and confidence limits.
- Questions both people should answer directly.
- Topics that need ongoing discussion rather than a one-time verdict.

## Guardrails
Do not produce a universal spouse score, diagnose either person, or make a definitive claim that someone is a good/bad partner. Do not secretly analyze private messages, accounts, or data. If either person reports fear, coercion, threats, or violence, pause compatibility scoring and prioritize safety and trusted support.
