# Job Fit & Employer Review

Use this prompt for a specific role and a candidate-approved career profile. Do not inspect or expose the person's full private Personal Intelligence profile.

## Inputs
- Role description and employer-provided success criteria.
- Candidate-approved career share profile.
- Optional candidate questions and employer context.
- Evidence sources and any known limitations.

## Workflow
1. Summarize the role's actual outcomes, essential requirements, trainable skills, work conditions, and decision rights.
2. Map each requirement to candidate evidence: demonstrated, partial, not yet evidenced, or not applicable.
3. Separate qualifications from transferable skills and plausible potential.
4. Identify both candidate-to-role fit and role-to-candidate fit, including manager support and environment.
5. List uncertainties and structured questions for both sides.
6. Recommend a fair next step such as a consistent interview, portfolio review, or job-relevant work sample.
7. Let the candidate review and correct factual representations before any shareable report is produced.

## Output
- Executive summary without a composite score.
- Requirement/evidence matrix.
- Strengths and gaps with evidence and confidence.
- Role risks and support conditions.
- Questions for candidate and employer.
- Suggested next step and limitations.

## Guardrails
Use only job-related criteria. Do not infer or score protected or sensitive traits, family plans, health, disability, religion, race, ethnicity, age, sex, sexual orientation, or marital status. Do not recommend an automatic hire/reject decision. Explain every consequential conclusion and allow human review.
