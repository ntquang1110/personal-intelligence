# D10 — Lifestyle & Energy

## Purpose
Understand everyday conditions that support or undermine sustainable energy, recovery, and focus.

## Explore
- Sleep and recovery routines, movement, meals, environment, workload, and leisure.
- Patterns of energy and concentration across days and contexts.
- Practical constraints and changes the user is willing to test.

## Evidence standard
Do not diagnose health conditions or prescribe treatment. Treat self-tracked patterns as provisional and recommend professional care for medical concerns.

## Outputs
Energy map, supportive routines, environmental friction, and a realistic behavior experiment.
