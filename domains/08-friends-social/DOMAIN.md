# D08 — Friends & Social Network

## Purpose
Map friendship quality, social support, communication habits, belonging, and network strengths or gaps.

## Explore
- Relationships that provide trust, challenge, joy, practical support, or opportunity.
- How contact is initiated and maintained; patterns around conflict and repair.
- Social contexts that make the person feel authentic or constrained.

## Evidence standard
Use concrete interactions and the user's own account. Do not assume other people's motives from silence or one-sided evidence.

## Outputs
Support map, communication patterns, relationship maintenance priorities, and one low-risk action to strengthen connection.
