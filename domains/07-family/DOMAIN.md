# D07 — Family

## Purpose
Understand family roles, expectations, support, responsibilities, communication, and boundaries from the user's perspective.

## Explore
- Important relationships and recurring interaction patterns.
- Caregiving, financial or practical responsibilities, expectations, and conflict.
- What the user can control, influence, accept, or needs to clarify.

## Evidence standard
Describe reported behavior and context without diagnosing or confidently labeling absent people. Respect privacy and safety; avoid assuming family structures or obligations.

## Outputs
Relationship needs, boundary options, communication experiments, support map, and unresolved questions.
