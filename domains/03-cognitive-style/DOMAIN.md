# D03 — Cognitive Style & Decisions

## Purpose
Understand how the person learns, reasons, notices information, chooses under uncertainty, and responds to stress.

## Explore
- Learning preferences supported by examples and outcomes.
- Decision speed, information thresholds, risk perception, and common biases.
- Response to ambiguity, disagreement, pressure, and cognitive overload.

## Evidence standard
Prefer multiple decisions across different contexts. Compare intended process with actual process and outcomes. Avoid unsupported IQ, diagnostic, or personality scoring.

## Outputs
Useful thinking conditions, likely decision traps, counter-strategies, and small tests that can validate the hypotheses.
