# D04 — Behavior & Execution

## Purpose
Identify the gap between intentions and repeated behavior, including habits, attention, follow-through, and energy management.

## Explore
- How work starts, stalls, finishes, and gets reviewed.
- Environmental triggers, friction, routines, interruptions, and accountability.
- Differences between high-performance and low-performance contexts.

## Evidence standard
Use specific recent examples and observable measures where possible. Avoid moralizing labels such as lazy or undisciplined; inspect system design and constraints too.

## Outputs
Behavior loops, execution bottlenecks, environmental changes, and one measurable experiment.
