# D06 — Career & Work

## Purpose
Find work environments, roles, and value propositions that fit demonstrated capabilities, motives, constraints, and market needs.

## Explore
- Tasks that create energy versus drain it; autonomy, pace, people, and ambiguity.
- Outcomes delivered, stakeholder value, leadership style, and marketable skills.
- Compensation needs, risk capacity, preferred working conditions, and opportunity costs.

## Evidence standard
Separate personal fit hypotheses from external market evidence. Treat career recommendations as options to test, not guarantees.

## Outputs
Role hypotheses, environment fit, capability gaps, market tests, and a prioritized career experiment.
