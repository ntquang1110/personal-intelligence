# D09 — Finance & Resources

## Purpose
Understand the resources and constraints that shape choices: money, time, attention, skills, assets, and risk capacity.

## Explore
- Income stability, obligations, buffers, spending patterns, and financial goals when voluntarily shared.
- Time allocation, attention fragmentation, tools, access, and support.
- Trade-offs between security, flexibility, growth, and optionality.

## Evidence standard
Use approximate ranges when exact figures are unnecessary. Do not request account credentials or expose sensitive records. Financial analysis is decision support, not a guarantee.

## Outputs
Resource map, binding constraints, risk boundaries, and a prioritized improvement opportunity.
