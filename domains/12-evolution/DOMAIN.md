# D12 — Evolution & Progress

## Purpose
Track what changes, what remains stable, and whether interventions produce meaningful improvement.

## Explore
- Baselines, experiments, milestones, setbacks, and lessons.
- Changes in confidence backed by new evidence; outdated conclusions to revisit.
- Progress signals that reflect outcomes rather than activity alone.

## Evidence standard
Use dates and comparable measures when available. Avoid cherry-picking wins or interpreting every setback as a trait.

## Outputs
Progress review, profile changes with evidence references, next priorities, and a decision to continue, adapt, or stop each experiment.
