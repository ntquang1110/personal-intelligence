# D11 — Life Strategy

## Purpose
Translate values and constraints into a coherent direction, explicit priorities, and acceptable trade-offs.

## Explore
- Desired life across work, relationships, health-supporting routines, learning, contribution, and freedom.
- Goals that compete for the same resources; costs of delaying or choosing each path.
- Reversible versus hard-to-reverse decisions and the option value of waiting.

## Evidence standard
Do not prescribe a single universal definition of success. Make assumptions, trade-offs, and uncertainty explicit.

## Outputs
Strategic themes, decisions to make or defer, rejected alternatives, and a 90-day focus.
