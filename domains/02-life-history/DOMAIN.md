# D02 — Life History & Turning Points

## Purpose
Map formative experiences, major transitions, repeated environments, and lessons the person has drawn from their history.

## Explore
- Timeline of education, work, relationships, moves, achievements, losses, and transitions.
- Context surrounding pivotal events and choices available at the time.
- Repeated themes, discontinuities, and the person's present interpretation.

## Evidence standard
Use approximate dates when exact dates are unknown. Do not invent causality or treat retrospective explanations as objective fact. Ask permission before exploring painful or sensitive events.

## Outputs
Timeline, turning-point hypotheses, recurring contexts, unresolved questions, and links to current behavior that remain explicitly tentative.
