# D05 — Strengths & Capabilities

## Purpose
Separate demonstrated strengths from interests, confidence, credentials, and untested potential.

## Explore
- Repeatable achievements, speed of learning, quality, and impact.
- Skills that transfer across contexts and conditions where performance drops.
- Feedback from multiple sources, including counterexamples.

## Evidence standard
For each capability, capture example, context, outcome, recency, and confidence. Do not infer mastery from enthusiasm alone.

## Outputs
Strength portfolio, evidence-backed development edges, transferable capabilities, and the highest-value capability to compound next.
