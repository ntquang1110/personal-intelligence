# D01 — Identity, Values & Motives

## Purpose
Clarify how the person sees themselves, what matters, what motivates them, and where stated values differ from observed choices.

## Explore
- Values ranked by demonstrated trade-offs, not just aspirational labels.
- Sources of motivation, pride, frustration, meaning, and autonomy.
- Self-concept, roles, identity tensions, and changes over time.

## Evidence standard
Record concrete examples, dates/context, and counterexamples. Distinguish self-report from observed behavior and interpretation. Do not turn a single episode into a fixed trait.

## Outputs
A provisional identity summary; ranked values with behavioral evidence; motive hypotheses; unresolved tensions; one question that would most improve confidence.
