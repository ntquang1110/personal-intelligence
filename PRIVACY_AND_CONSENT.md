# Privacy, Consent & Shareable Profiles

## Core rule
The full Personal Intelligence profile is private by default. Sharing is a separate, explicit action for a specific audience and purpose. Never expose the canonical profile, evidence ledger, family notes, financial details, or private relationship reflections wholesale.

## Separate views
- **Private self view:** full personal workspace controlled by the user.
- **Career share view:** user-approved role-related skills, outcomes, portfolio evidence, work preferences, and relevant constraints.
- **Partner share view:** user-approved values, relationship intentions, life expectations, communication preferences, and discussion topics.
- **Mutual review view:** only the fields both parties agreed to compare.

Do not assume that consent for one audience implies consent for another.

## Consent record
Before sharing, record:
- who is sharing and with whom;
- purpose and intended audience;
- exact fields and attachments included;
- creation and expiry/review date;
- whether downloading or onward sharing is allowed;
- how the person can withdraw consent;
- what remains excluded.

If the runtime cannot enforce expiry, access controls, or deletion, state that limitation clearly. A Markdown file is not an access-control system.

## Data minimization
- Share the smallest amount of information needed for the stated decision.
- Prefer summaries and references to raw records.
- Exclude passwords, API keys, identity numbers, private messages, precise financial account details, and other people's personal information.
- Never add personal profiles or completed reviews to a public repository.
- Do not use sensitive personal information as job-fit criteria.
- For partner matching, sensitive topics should be discussed directly and voluntarily rather than inferred or secretly collected.

## Review and withdrawal
Let the owner inspect the exact shareable version before export. Include a review date and a simple way to revoke future access. Explain that revocation cannot recall screenshots, copies, or information already seen. Keep a local record of what was shared, with whom, and when, without unnecessarily retaining the recipient's private notes.

## Third-party feedback
Label feedback as attributed, anonymous, or unattributed; record context and date where appropriate. Do not silently blend another person's opinion into objective fact. Allow the subject to respond or contest the interpretation. Avoid collecting feedback from people who are not authorized to provide it.

## Fairness and human agency
Matching outputs must be explainable, contestable, and non-final. Do not infer protected characteristics or use opaque scores to exclude candidates or judge partners. Every person can decline to share, disagree with the analysis, or decide not to proceed.
