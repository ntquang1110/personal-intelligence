# Synthetic Example Report — Not a Real User Assessment

## Observation
A hypothetical user reports beginning several online courses and completing few.

## Hypothesis
The user may struggle to sustain self-directed learning projects.

## Counterevidence and alternatives
- Courses may have been exploratory by design.
- Completion may not be the user's chosen success criterion.
- Workload, course quality, or changing priorities may explain the pattern.

## Low-risk test
For 30 days, select one course linked to a concrete goal. Define a completion artifact, schedule two study blocks weekly, and record whether the blocks occur and what is produced.

## Limitation
No conclusion about discipline, motivation, or personality is justified from the observation alone.
